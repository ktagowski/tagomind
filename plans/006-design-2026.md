# 006 — Design refresh toward a 2026/27 feel

Written after a blind `ultra-conservative` review of all nine page types (2026-10-29). Scores before: Home 46, Publications 45, Paper 45, Research 40, Project 32, Products 30, About 28, Services 21, Projects 18. Gold standards G1–G12 must stay green; no signature motion is removed (docs/motion.md).

## What the review found dated

- Chrome: full-width top bar 16px wider than the content column on each side; bordered active-nav pill; bordered theme square beside a navy pill CTA.
- Colour: a generic navy accent competing with each project's plate colour (two accent systems on every project page); field hues that collide (three purples, two greens).
- The SaaS card kit: every item a bordered, shadowed card; icon-in-tinted-square feature cards with a gradient wash; uppercase mono venue pills; rows of up to eight identical outline pills; boxes inside boxes (projects toolbar).
- Atmosphere: a blue radial wash behind every page title; a brand wash plus dot grid on the footer.

## Plan

**Colour.** Ink UI, colour from content. The default accent becomes `mono` (ink); plates, paper figures and field dots are the only hue. One signal green marks live status. `?accent=blue` still restores the old navy for comparison.

**Type.** Fraunces (display, optical size up to 144) and Inter stay. Display tracking tightens; the hero's italic phrase is ink, a change of voice, not a colour accent. Venue labels are sentence-set sans, never wrapped.

**Material.** Glass only for floating chrome: the top bar becomes an island aligned to the content column, and the Explorer toolbar is one glass bar with borderless fields. Cards lose the grey border-plus-shadow for a hairline ring and a soft ambient shadow; radii are concentric and use `corner-shape: squircle` where supported.

**Layout.** Lists are unboxed: publications are hairline-separated rows with compact text links; feature cards drop the icon tile and wash. Page-title glows become a neutral top light (same element, same motion). The footer is one quiet surface with aligned, equal-sized social glyphs.

**Bugs to fix** (Phase B of the review): footer social icons squashed by `.footer-col a { width: fit-content }`; header vs content edges; white photo block in dark mode; publications author highlight missing its class; year rules in two colours under `?still=1`; native select chevron; wrapping venue badge; card context line floating; study-mark arrow pushed to the column edge; mobile paper eyebrow halo and dash indent; services gutters and wrapping CTA row; band widow; ragged stacked buttons on phones; live badge pulsing forever (docs/motion.md asks for three loops).

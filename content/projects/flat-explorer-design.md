## From a brief to an editable scene

The Design Lab turns a conversation into a structured room brief: dimensions, openings, furniture, positions, and finishes. The brief remains editable before scene generation.

The generator produces a scene specification that is checked for dimensional consistency, wall fit, openings, and overlaps. A renderer builds the 3D scene from that specification. Versions keep the brief, scene, screenshots, and review together so changes can be inspected.

## Inside the workspace

Room viewers expose dimensions, alternative camera views, and furniture details. Kitchen controls include cabinet-front colours and countertop finishes. Plans and visual references sit alongside the interactive scenes.

## Availability

The online design workspace is password-protected. The AI creator and its generation workflow run in the local Design Lab; they are not offered as a public self-service feature.

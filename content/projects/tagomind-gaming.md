## Why a studio

Even a simple mobile game costs tens of thousands of dollars before anyone plays it, and most launched games never earn that back. Small, odd worlds stay on paper. TagoMind Gaming builds its own tools to cut what a game costs to make, and spends the difference on worlds players want to explore.

## The platform

The first game started as a side project. The pipeline built to ship it is becoming the TagoMind Gaming platform: AI writes the code, runs the builds and tests, and checks the balance maths. The art, the story and every decision stay with people.

## Status

The first title is in closed testing for iPhone with a small group of players. Its name and first look arrive on reveal day, planned for November 2026, with an App Store release to follow. Dates are expected and may move.

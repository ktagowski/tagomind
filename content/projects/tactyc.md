TACTYC (Toss A Coin to A Cartographer) is an active research project on AI for generating and editing game maps.

## Research focus

The work connects vision-language models that read terrain tile by tile with generative models that redraw it, studying map understanding, generation, and editing.

## My role

- **Coordination.** Planned the research as tasks for the student team, from model reviews and the proof of concept to the evaluation data.
- **Engineering.** Wrote most of the codebase: LoRA fine-tuning of diffusion models in three variants, the FastAPI backend, and the Next.js chat app.

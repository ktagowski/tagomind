How to play ↓

01The rule

★ tyres only. No exceptions.

Customers walk into the bay carrying a tyre for the M340i. Only tyres with the ★ on the sidewall go on that car. Dex fits them and hits the rest. You decide who gets which.

★ on the sidewall · OK

Let them in.

Leave them alone. When they reach the car Dex fits the tyre: +50, and the clock refills.

No ★ · Beat

Take them down.

Click or tap them before they reach the car. Every clean hit scores and keeps your streak going.

02How to play

Four things to know.

  1. 01Click the bayAny click or tap on the scene starts a round. Esc leaves, R plays again.
  2. 02Read the sidewallPoint at a customer and a loupe shows the tyre up close: ★ OK or NO ★ · BEAT.
  3. 03Hit only the cheap onesA click on empty forecourt breaks your streak. Hitting a ★ customer costs a life.
  4. 04Three lives, one clockA wrong hit or a no-★ tyre on the car costs a life. The clock pauses while nobody needs a beating.
